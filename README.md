### 🍹 What I'm building: Vita Hub

**Vita Hub** is the cloud operations system that runs Vita Fruity, my multi-outlet juice and food business. It replaced our off-the-shelf point-of-sale (POS) software and is used daily by cashiers, managers, factory staff and the back office.

- **Point of sale:** offline-capable till, receipts, split payments, refunds, manager approvals and end-of-day reports
- **Online ordering:** website and Android app with payments, delivery tracking, loyalty points and stamp cards
- **Factory and stores:** production, supplies, recipes, requisitions, stock counts and inter-outlet transfers
- **HR and payroll:** GPS clock-in, leave, payslips, staff savings and duty rosters
- **Reports:** sales, profit & loss, stock reconciliation and approval workflows

**Built with:** JavaScript (Node.js), PostgreSQL / Supabase, Netlify serverless functions, Capacitor (Android / iOS), and payment gateway integrations.

*The code is private because it runs a live business. I'm happy to walk through it.*
